About Leo Batavus

Leo Batavus follows the news, the politics and the Brussels file of the Netherlands by one question: what does this mean for the Netherlands?

It writes from the Dutch national centre, soberly and without an ideology: Dutch interests first, Europe where it pays. It is an independent publication and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any party or movement.

Articles here are drafted with AI from published sources. An editor checks most of them before they appear; an article that went out unchecked says so. Illustrations are generated with AI. Leo Batavus is independent and speaks for no party or movement.
